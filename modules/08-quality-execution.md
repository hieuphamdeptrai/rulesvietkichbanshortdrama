# AI Execution, Red-Team, Metrics, Simulation & Mutation
**Origins:** v2.1, v2.2, v2.3, v2.6, v2.7

## Execution
- Priority: logic→curiosity→character→escalation→payoff→pacing→dialogue→polish.
- Modes: Discovery/Concept/Blueprint/Episode Planning/Screenplay/Audit/Rewrite.
- No premature canon screenplay; samples are NON-CANON.
- Constraint-first; assumptions separate; Writer Truth separate from Audience Information.
- Before writing, load character knowledge/current state; simulate antagonist response.
- Generate in passes and maintain timeline/scene/info/subtext/production checks.

## Red-Team & scoring
- Writer Mode cannot self-approve major work.
- Lenses: Bored Viewer, Smart Viewer, Skeptic, Mystery Fan, Character Viewer, Continuity Editor, Producer.
- Attack logic, obvious solution, competence, POV fairness, predictability, randomness, necessity, consequence, repetition, hook/cliff/middle/finale.
- Scores use 0/1/2 with evidence; Critical Override and weak-link floor beat averages.
- Metrics can include theory/trust volatility, Q progression, reward/debt/payoff, filler/repetition/coincidence/agency/villain competence/emotion/rewatch/spoiler/production.

## Audience simulation
- Track Knowledge/Belief/Suspicion/Questions/Expectation/Trust/Emotion.
- Personas: Attentive/Casual/Theory Hunter/Emotional/Skeptic/Binge.
- No Writer Truth leak. Track theory monopoly, boredom, frustration, memory decay/reactivation, clarity and writer trust.

## Mutation
- Diagnose symptom→root cause→target.
- Preserve strengths; Minimum-Change Rule.
- Axes WHO/WHAT/WHY/HOW/WHEN/WHERE/RELATIONSHIP/KNOWLEDGE/COST/MEANING.
- Conservative/Balanced/Bold options must differ structurally.
- Reframe before retcon. Validate ripple and regressions.
- Stop when target function restored; do not endlessly optimize.

# Escalation, Conflict, Payoff & Satisfaction
**Origins:** v1.7, v1.8

- Mystery must create consequence, choice, loss, danger, conflict or time pressure.
- Escalation axes: Danger / Cost / Time / Information / Relationship / Moral.
- Price of Knowledge: learning truth increasingly costs something.
- Deadlines must arise from story logic.
- Trust can be stake/resource; use No-Good Choice and Pressure Triangle.
- Objectives shift after major reveal; antagonist adapts; protagonist errors come from flaw/incomplete info, not stupidity.
- Success With Cost / Failure With Value; no reset outcomes.
- Stakes may deepen personal→relationship→identity→system→moral.
- Midpoint combines reveal + pressure and changes objective; 75% phase creates meaningful crash/loss.
- Payoff applies to question/clue/promise/relationship/behavior/line/object/choice/arc.
- Setup weight should match payoff weight.
- Strong payoff: Answer→Meaning→Consequence.
- Rewards include answer/twist/catharsis/theory confirmation/reframe/justice/tragedy.
- Maintain micro/mid/arc/core cadence, callbacks and Payoff Debt Ledger.
- Character/relationship payoff should often occur through final choice.
- Finale leaves enough time after truth for consequence/catharsis; core answer covers WHO/WHAT/WHY/HOW/WHEN/WHY NOW/WHY PROTAGONIST as relevant.
- Main story closure precedes sequel bait.

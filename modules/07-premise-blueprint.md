# Premise Forge & Series Blueprint
**Origins:** v2.0, v2.8

Premise = Protagonist + Unusual Situation + Personal Stake + Curiosity Gap + Hidden Truth Potential.

- Five premise blocks: WHO / WHAT WRONG / WHY MATTER / WHY CAN'T LEAVE / WHAT TRUTH COULD BE.
- High concept is quickly understandable and immediately question-generating.
- Story Lock explains why protagonist cannot simply leave.
- Answer Why This Protagonist and Why Now.
- Use Contradiction Core / Impossible Detail when useful; Question Stack = Immediate / Personal / Deep.
- Primary curiosity engine: Identity/Motive/Cause/Relationship/System/Time/Self.
- Run One-Line, And-Then, Why-Not-Leave, 30-episode fuel, escalation, payoff, theory-space, production and spoiler-resilience tests.
- Avoid One-Twist Premise; engine survives the first reveal.
- Build backward from Final Truth.
- Define Audience False Belief and truth layers Surface→False→Midpoint→Near-Final→Core.
- Blueprint includes Mystery Ladder, arc cards, character/secret/trust/suspect maps, clue/red-herring/payoff/twist banks, escalation/resources, major events and Points of No Return.
- THEREFORE/BUT causality; test finale before fully committing Episode 1.
- Blueprint Lock freezes required contracts and records flexible zones.

# PROJECT SEED

- Working title: The Wedding Room
- Core Concept: A bride hears someone outside her wedding room use a private nickname only her dead father knew.
- Protagonist: newly married bride in an arranged marriage.
- Primary Curiosity: who knows the nickname and why?
- Story Lock: leaving the marriage would endanger her family.
- Genre: Historical Romance Mystery (provisional)
- Format: 60–90s episodes (provisional)
- Constraint: grounded; no supernatural solution.

## Mandatory escalation
- Premise/Core Truth/Blueprint **lock**.
- Episode or Screenplay containing a major reveal, twist, betrayal, irreversible choice, core clue/payoff, or canon-sensitive continuity.
- Audit, certification, rescue, major mutation, or branch merge.
- Any ambiguity where a compact summary may have omitted a condition, exception, ban, score, or failure mode.

## Lightweight mode
Prototype/non-canon ideation can normally use compact modules only.

## Rule
Compact summaries may never be used as justification to silently ignore a frozen detailed rule. History is loaded selectively as an operational reference, not dumped wholesale.

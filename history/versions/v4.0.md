# v4.0 — STYLE, VOICE & CREATIVE IDENTITY ENGINE

**Frozen design history.** Runtime consolidation does not delete these rules.

**Current operational owner:** `modules/09-genre-format-style.md`

## Frozen rules
- **H-40-001** — Style controls how the audience experiences story; it is not story content and not decoration alone.
- **H-40-002** — Creative identity combines story promise, emotional temperature, voice, rhythm, visual language, motifs, restraint rules, and signature devices.
- **H-40-003** — Every project should define a Style Profile covering tonal core, dialogue, narration, emotional intensity, realism, poeticism, humor, violence presentation, visual language, motifs, and forbidden drift.
- **H-40-004** — Tonal core must be described operationally, not with a vague adjective such as “dark”.
- **H-40-005** — Tone may be modeled across Darkness/Warmth/Humor/Romanticism/Brutality/Melodrama/Realism/Stylization/Poeticism/Emotional Exposure.
- **H-40-006** — Define an emotional-temperature baseline (cold/warm/hot) and intentional deviations.
- **H-40-007** — Style uses baseline + allowed deviations + forbidden drift.
- **H-40-008** — Dialogue DNA should define directness, sentence length, literalness/subtext, emotional exposure, formality, sharpness/naturalism, and exposition level.
- **H-40-009** — Project voice and individual character voice are separate layers and both must remain coherent.
- **H-40-010** — Character Voice Profile should track vocabulary, sentence length, directness, emotional exposure, formality, favorite tactic, lie style, silence, anger, and vulnerability.
- **H-40-011** — Character voice must grow from psychology rather than arbitrary gimmicks.
- **H-40-012** — Main characters should be differentiated by thought pattern and communication strategy, not catchphrases alone.
- **H-40-013** — Removing speaker names should often leave enough voice distinction to infer the speaker, though not every line must be identifiable.
- **H-40-014** — Thought pattern drives speech pattern.
- **H-40-015** — Power context changes how a character speaks without erasing the underlying voice.
- **H-40-016** — Major relationships may have a shared dialogue dynamic that evolves with relationship state.
- **H-40-017** — Changes in address, formality, intimacy, directness, and silence can become relationship payoffs.
- **H-40-018** — Vocabulary/register must remain consistent with world, period, social rank, and relationship.
- **H-40-019** — Maintain a terminology/style glossary for titles, address forms, world terms, prohibited modern phrases, and recurring objects when relevant.
- **H-40-020** — Dialogue-length profile should fit short-drama economy; monologues are deliberate exceptions, not default exposition containers.
- **H-40-021** — Project should define whether monologues belong to its style and when they are permitted.
- **H-40-022** — Prefer exposition mechanisms consistent with project identity; do not default to direct explanation.
- **H-40-023** — Narration must define narrator identity, reliability, tense, distance, poetic level, function, temperature, and density.
- **H-40-024** — Narration may compress, frame perspective/emotion, create irony, bridge time, or echo theme.
- **H-40-025** — Narration must not be used to patch missing motivation, continuity, plot holes, or scene logic.
- **H-40-026** — Character narrators obey the same knowledge-continuity rules as dialogue characters.
- **H-40-027** — Narration and visual/dialogue should not redundantly state the same fact unless deliberate effect requires it.
- **H-40-028** — Visual language is recurring visual grammar, not a shot list.
- **H-40-029** — Define framing tendency, camera energy, composition, lighting logic, detail emphasis, reveal presentation, and emotional distance.
- **H-40-030** — Camera energy may be static/floating/handheld/mixed with project-specific rules.
- **H-40-031** — Reaction-before-information versus information-before-reaction can be a consistent reveal style.
- **H-40-032** — Major reveals should follow a project-consistent presentation style unless deviation is purposeful.
- **H-40-033** — Motifs may be object/image/sound/phrase/gesture/location/color/composition and should accumulate or change meaning.
- **H-40-034** — Motif and clue are distinct; an element may serve both functions.
- **H-40-035** — Motif lifecycle: Plant→Repeat→Associate→Change Meaning→Payoff.
- **H-40-036** — Do not saturate motifs so heavily that they become branding gimmicks.
- **H-40-037** — Sound motifs and sound-language rules may carry threat, memory, transition, or clue function.
- **H-40-038** — Music philosophy should define whether music leads emotion or remains restrained; avoid scoring every emotion at maximum intensity.
- **H-40-039** — Silence may replace answers/confessions/reactions/threats when its meaning remains readable.
- **H-40-040** — Rhythm DNA defines how pacing feels: staccato/suspended/flowing/contrasted, while the pacing engine still controls progress.
- **H-40-041** — Different scene functions may use different rhythm profiles within a coherent project baseline.
- **H-40-042** — Emotional-expression style defines whether emotion is shown through speech, action, withdrawal, anger, humor, ritual, or other project-consistent behavior.
- **H-40-043** — Show/say balance and emotional restraint should fit genre and project identity without making emotion unreadable.
- **H-40-044** — Melodrama should control simultaneous intensity of dialogue, performance, music, and visual presentation rather than maxing every layer.
- **H-40-045** — Comedy may humanize or release tension but must not erase serious consequence unless genre intentionally permits it.
- **H-40-046** — Project humor should have a defined source and not turn every character into the same witty voice.
- **H-40-047** — Violence presentation should be defined (implied/impact-focused/graphic/stylized/consequence-focused) and serious violence must persist in state.
- **H-40-048** — Romance style should define how intimacy is expressed through dialogue, service, touch, sacrifice, vulnerability, address, or other signals.
- **H-40-049** — Realism profile (grounded/heightened/stylized/mythic) changes audience expectations but never removes internal logic requirements.
- **H-40-050** — Poeticism and symbolism density should be deliberately controlled; not every line or prop should become poetic/symbolic.
- **H-40-051** — Project should ideally have a compact Signature Stack: dialogue signature, visual signature, emotional signature, motif signature.
- **H-40-052** — Signature devices must serve story/emotion/meaning/curiosity, not decoration alone.
- **H-40-053** — Maintain a Creative Identity Card with Story Promise/Tonal Core/Temperature/Dialogue/Narration/Visual Grammar/Rhythm/Motifs/Signature/Forbidden Drift.
- **H-40-054** — Forbidden Style Drift rules are project-specific and should protect identity without freezing character growth.
- **H-40-055** — Important style invariants may be locked, but style is generally softer than canon and allows intentional exceptions.
- **H-40-056** — Record significant style deviations with reason so contrast becomes meaningful rather than accidental drift.
- **H-40-057** — Style itself may arc over the series when it expresses character or thematic change.
- **H-40-058** — Arc- or POV-level modifiers may vary atmosphere while inheriting global project identity.
- **H-40-059** — Compiler should build task-specific Style Packs rather than load the entire Style Bible for every task.
- **H-40-060** — Dialogue context should combine project style + character voices + relationship state + emotional state + scene function.
- **H-40-061** — Style profile is high-salience project memory; retrieve only the relevant subset.
- **H-40-062** — Style validation should flag voice drift, project tone drift, exposition spikes, narration patches, motif saturation, signature decay, voice convergence, register breaks, overexposure, and consequence-reset tone.
- **H-40-063** — Use a Voice Editor/Style Red-Team lens to test whether the series feels authored rather than generic AI output.
- **H-40-064** — Watch for generic AI dialogue patterns: uniformly polished sentences, constant explanation, repeated dramatic formulas, rhetorical-question excess, generic emotional phrases, identical cadence.
- **H-40-065** — Project may maintain an overused-phrase list; familiar lines are not universally banned but should gain specificity/context.
- **H-40-066** — Specific dialogue tied to concrete facts is generally stronger than generic accusation or sentiment.
- **H-40-067** — Character-specific metaphor should arise from that character’s world and experience.
- **H-40-068** — Screenplay action prose should be visual, compressed, playable, and not overloaded with novelistic interiority or unnecessary camera direction.
- **H-40-069** — Separate Story Style, Screenplay Style, and Production Style; connect them without conflating them.
- **H-40-070** — Style cascade: Project Style→Arc Modifier→Character Voice→Relationship Dynamic→Scene State→Line/Shot execution.
- **H-40-071** — Important confession/death/betrayal/final-confrontation scenes may receive explicit Scene Style or Reveal Style Cards.
- **H-40-072** — Style may strengthen curiosity through indirect dialogue, reaction withholding, motif, and visual contradiction, but must not create confusion.
- **H-40-073** — Stylistic behavior can become clue or payoff when it changes meaning.
- **H-40-074** — Production feasibility constrains execution of style; prefer signatures that can be reproduced consistently in AI production.
- **H-40-075** — v3.7 may branch tonal/screenplay/production executions without automatically changing story canon.
- **H-40-076** — Basic style identity should be soft-locked after concept/genre and sufficiently locked before batch screenplay generation; visual style before bulk asset generation.
- **H-40-077** — Style observability should monitor voice consistency, motif saturation, signature decay, exposition drift, and related trends.
- **H-40-078** — Project-specific Style Tests may enforce story-relevant voice/address/narration rules.
- **H-40-079** — When style becomes generic, diagnose which signature function disappeared instead of adding arbitrary poetic language.
- **H-40-080** — Style mutation is a low-ripple way to refresh execution only after story logic is sound.
- **H-40-081** — Content-before-expression priority remains: logic→character→curiosity→style.
- **H-40-082** — Bootstrap should include a compact Style Pack so new models/sessions preserve creative identity.
- **H-40-083** — Translation must preserve functionally equivalent power, intimacy, formality, subtext, and voice rather than literal wording.
- **H-40-084** — Do not copy the surface style of references; decompose references into principles and synthesize an original project identity.
- **H-40-085** — Style QA belongs after logic/character/pacing passes, not before them.
- **H-40-086** — Do not preserve a beautiful line that violates character logic.
- **H-40-087** — Human-sounding imperfection must emerge from character pressure rather than random corruption of dialogue.
- **H-40-088** — Subtext test: what a line says and what the character wants should not be identical in every line; directness can become payoff after long avoidance.
- **H-40-089** — Style contrast gains meaning only when a baseline has been established.
- **H-40-090** — Final Style principle: plot creates next-event curiosity, character creates choice curiosity, style makes the audience recognize the project’s world and creative identity.

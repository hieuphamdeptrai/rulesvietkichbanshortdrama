# v1.9 — PACING & RETENTION ENGINE

**Frozen design history.** Runtime consolidation does not delete these rules.

**Current operational owner:** `modules/02-episode-pacing.md`

## Frozen rules
- **H-19-001** — Pacing is not speed; it is perceived progress.
- **H-19-002** — Story State Change includes Knowledge / Trust / Danger / Objective / Power / Relationship.
- **H-19-003** — Short-form guideline: meaningful new information/action/suspicion/visual/threat/clue/reversal roughly every 8–15s.
- **H-19-004** — Rehook roughly every 15–25s when useful.
- **H-19-005** — Use tension waves rather than constant maximum intensity.
- **H-19-006** — Present one major idea at a time.
- **H-19-007** — Allow roughly 1–5s processing after major moments when format allows.
- **H-19-008** — Dialogue carries micro-pressure; remove filler.
- **H-19-009** — Start late and leave early.
- **H-19-010** — Retention Chain: Hook→Q→Action→Clue→Theory→Complication→Reveal→NewQ→Cliff.
- **H-19-011** — Use Question Handoff.
- **H-19-012** — Information tax categories: Must / Nice / Later.
- **H-19-013** — Compression pass can target ~20% reduction while protecting major reveal.
- **H-19-014** — Measure Story Payload per scene/episode.
- **H-19-015** — Use Drop Detector where nothing meaningful changes.
- **H-19-016** — Recap should be minimal and purposeful.
- **H-19-017** — Slow down major reveal.
- **H-19-018** — Slow down major emotional choice/death/final payoff.
- **H-19-019** — Retention does not mean constant twist.
- **H-19-020** — Midpoint resets engine.
- **H-19-021** — Final phase converges engine.
- **H-19-022** — Audience should always feel closer to something meaningful.
